## Regulation & operations (round 2)
- GPSR (EU) 2023/988 applies since 13 Dec 2024: every product needs an EU-based responsible economic operator; online offers must show manufacturer/responsible person and safety info. Applies to dropshippers. [S] https://ecommercegermany.com/blog/gpsr-general-product-safety-regulation/ · https://www.obelis.net/news/new-regulation-for-general-products-applies-from-december-2024/
- Cosmetics (EC) 1223/2009: EU Responsible Person (written mandate), Cosmetic Product Safety Report by a qualified assessor, CPNP notification BEFORE sale. No public price found. [S] https://www.cosmeticsandtoiletries.com/regulations/regional/blog/21837694/eu-regulation-no-1223-2009-part-iii-the-responsible-person · https://www.obelis.net/blog/the-cpnp-notification-process-step-by-step/
- Greece EPR (ν. 4819/2021): producers/importers of packaged products register in ΕΜΠΑ (ΕΟΑΝ); pure resellers (διακινητές) are not obliged; fines €100–500,000; ΓΕΜΗ cross-check (ν. 5151/2024). Role of an e-shop that imports goods must be checked with accountant/ΕΟΑΝ. [S] https://www.odigostoupoliti.eu/ethniko-mitroo-paragogon-empa/ · https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/5151/2024/arthro/17
